# Malayalam Quest

[![License: GPL v2](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-GPL%20v2-blue.svg)](LICENSE)

A gamified, interactive app for learning the Malayalam script from zero — no prior knowledge needed.

## Features

- 🗺️ **RPG Adventure Map** — 10 chapters unlock progressively
- 📖 **4 Exercise Types** — Flashcard, Multiple Choice, Matching, Listen & Pick
- 🔊 **Native Audio** — Hear authentic Malayalam pronunciation
- ⭐ **XP & Titles** — Level up from Script Novice to Language Champion
- 🏆 **Chapter Badges** — Earn a badge on completing each chapter
- 💾 **Offline Progress** — Saved locally, no account needed

## Chapters

| # | Chapter | Content |
|---|---------|---------|
| 1 | ⚔️ The Vowel Kingdom | 13 vowels |
| 2 | 🌿 The Consonant Forest I | 15 consonants (ka, cha, ta groups) |
| 3 | 🔮 The Consonant Cave II | 21 consonants (dental, labial, sonorants) |
| 4 | 🏰 The Matra Citadel | 11 vowel signs |
| 5 | 👑 The Chillu Chamber | Chillu letters & visarga |
| 6 | 🌊 The Number Shore | Numerals & colours |
| 7 | 🌾 The Greeting Grove | Common words & greetings |
| 8 | 🌸 The Sentence Garden | Simple sentences |
| 9 | 🏔️ The Grammar Summit | Tense & postpositions |
| 10 | 🔥 The Reading Citadel | Reading passages |

## How to Play

Open the app, enter your name, and start with Chapter 1. Each lesson runs through 4 exercise types automatically. Complete all lessons in a chapter to earn its badge and unlock the next chapter.

## Deploy Your Own Copy

1. Fork this repository
2. Go to **Settings → Pages → Source → Deploy from branch: `main`**
3. Your app is live at `https://YOUR_USERNAME.github.io/REPO_NAME/`
